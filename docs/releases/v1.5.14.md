# Lumon 1.5.14

- Stop requiring repair-status rechecks for ignored Auto Scan issues. Keep ignored history available for semantic matching so equivalent new candidates retain the ignored choice and do not create duplicate Jira Bugs.
- Exclude ignored issues and newly matched ignored groups from required recheck validation. Missing or unsolicited assessments for these issues no longer mark an otherwise complete scan as incomplete or prevent new-finding completion hooks.
- Display recorded scan failure reasons directly under Current progress, with expandable details in scan history and an explicit fallback when no reason was recorded. Support English, Simplified Chinese and Traditional Chinese.
- Remove the validation-record block from issue details while preserving stored scan evidence and status updates.
- Show task titles without the Jira-key prefix in Delivery history, keeping the key in the metadata row and preserving clickable titles. Vertically center delivery-table cells.
- Add regression coverage for ignored identity matching, completion-hook eligibility, failure details, localization and delivery-history links.

This release does not rewrite historical scan receipts, retry scans or Jira hooks, change schedules, or alter Workspace configuration. Non-ignored incomplete rechecks and other scan failures retain the existing completion-hook gates. New behavior applies to future scans after upgrading.
